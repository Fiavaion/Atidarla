# Operator reference

Generated by `atidarla docs` from the operator registry. Do not edit by hand.

## IMG (45)

- [Analyze](img/analyze.md) — Measures the whole image on the GPU: average r, g, b, a and luminance, and the darkest and brightest luminance, as channels (a frame or two behind the image).
- [Blend](img/blend.md) — Crossfades from input A to input B.
- [Bloom](img/bloom.md) — Adds a glow around bright areas.
- [Blur](img/blur.md) — Gaussian blur.
- [Cache](img/cache.md) — Delays the input by a number of frames (frame history).
- [Channel Image](img/channel_image.md) — Draws SIG channels as an image: waveforms (samples along x, a row per channel) or band rows (channels along x). Feeds ISF audio inputs.
- [Channel Mix](img/channel_mix.md) — Rebuilds each output channel as a weighted sum of the input's R, G, B and A.
- [Chroma Key](img/chroma_key.md) — Makes pixels close to a key colour transparent.
- [Composite](img/composite.md) — Combines input A with input B using a blend operation.
- [Compute](img/compute.md) — A compute shader you write in WGSL: `fn kernel(id: vec2u)` runs once per output pixel. `#bind` lines declare the inputs (`in0`…`in3`), a storage `output` image, read-write `state` images that swap between iterations and persist, named parameters (`p1`…`p8`) and scratch buffers; Tile sets the workgroup size and Iterations how many dispatches run per cook. `time()`, `frame()`, `size()`, `iteration()` and sampler `samp` are available. A file reloads whenever it is saved; the last working shader keeps running while you edit. Reference: docs/compute.md.
- [Constant](img/constant.md) — Fills the image with one colour.
- [Crop](img/crop.md) — Keeps a rectangle of the input. By default the output is that rectangle at its own pixel size; `input` size stretches it to the input's size instead.
- [Displace](img/displace.md) — Offsets input A by input B: red moves horizontally, green vertically, around mid-grey.
- [Edge](img/edge.md) — Outlines: the strength of luminance edges (Sobel).
- [Feedback](img/feedback.md) — Outputs the previous frame of the `loop` input, closing a feedback loop; outputs `in` until the loop has a frame or while reset is on.
- [Fluid 2D](img/fluid.md) — An incompressible 2D fluid carrying coloured dye. A built-in emitter and the `dye` and `velocity` images stir it; Reset clears it.
- [HSV Adjust](img/hsv_adjust.md) — Rotates hue and scales saturation and value.
- [Kaleidoscope](img/kaleidoscope.md) — Folds the input into mirrored segments around the centre.
- [Level](img/level.md) — Brightness, contrast, gamma, opacity and invert.
- [Lookup](img/lookup.md) — Recolours input A through a lookup image B: each pixel's luminance picks a colour along B's horizontal centre line.
- [Luma Key](img/luma_key.md) — Makes pixels outside a luminance range transparent.
- [Mirror](img/mirror.md) — Reflects one half of the image onto the other.
- [Movie In](img/movie_in.md) — Plays a movie or still image. HAP, HAP Alpha, HAP Q and HAP R are decoded natively and upload as GPU-compressed textures; PNG and JPEG stills load as one frame; other codecs (H.264, HEVC, ProRes, VP9 and more) decode through FFmpeg, with hardware decoding where the platform has it, in builds with FFmpeg.
- [Neural CA](img/nca.md) — A neural cellular automaton: every cell perceives its neighbours through fixed Sobel and Laplacian filters and updates itself with a tiny neural network, at random but repeatable moments. The built-in rules (hand-built, not trained) grow a patterned organism from a seed and regrow what the `erase` image removes; a weights file in the project supplies any other network. `seed` can start it from an image. Reference: docs/nca.md.
- [Noise](img/noise.md) — Fractal gradient noise. Animate `z` to move through it over time.
- [Normal Map](img/normal_map.md) — Treats luminance as height and outputs tangent-space normals (RGB = XYZ × 0.5 + 0.5).
- [Projection](img/projection.md) — Converts between panoramic and camera projections: equirectangular (360°), fisheye, perspective and a cubemap cross, with a yaw/pitch/roll rotation.
- [Ramp](img/ramp.md) — A gradient between two colours: horizontal, vertical, radial or angular.
- [Reaction-Diffusion](img/reaction_diffusion.md) — A Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion simulation growing organic patterns from a seed; bright areas of the `seed` input add more.
- [Remap](img/remap.md) — Samples input A at the coordinates stored in input B's red and green channels.
- [Render](img/render.md) — Draws up to four geometries (`geo`, `geo2`…), each with the material wired beside it (`mat`, `mat2`…; a grey PBR surface coloured by `Cd` when none), through a perspective or orthographic camera, lit by one directional light and ambient light, with a depth buffer and antialiasing. Polygons are filled (normals from `N`, else faceted; texture coordinates from `uv`), polylines drawn as lines, and geometry without primitives as points.
- [Render Points](img/render_points.md) — Draws the points of a geometry as square sprites over a background, looking down the Z axis (orthographic). Colour from the `Cd` attribute or a constant; additive blending makes dense regions glow.
- [Render Points](img/render_points.md) — Draws the points of a geometry as square sprites over a background, looking down the Z axis (orthographic), and optionally its polyline primitives as one-pixel lines (such as Connect's). Colour from the `Cd` attribute or a constant; additive blending makes dense regions glow.
- [Resolution](img/resolution.md) — Resamples the input to another size: a fraction of the input (or of the project size when nothing is connected), or exact pixels. Operators after it work at the new size.
- [Ripple](img/ripple.md) — A water-surface simulation: bright areas of the `drive` input push the surface, and waves spread and fade.
- [Shape](img/shape.md) — A circle, rectangle or regular polygon with soft edges.
- [Share In](img/share_in.md) — Receives an image another application publishes on this computer (a Spout sender on Windows), or a Share Out's. The output takes the sender's size and follows it when it changes; while there is no sender it is black. Spout needs the DX12 graphics backend (elsewhere the node reports an error); Syphon on macOS is not wired yet.
- [Share Out](img/share_out.md) — Publishes its input to other applications on this computer (a Spout sender on Windows) and passes it through. Receivers get 8-bit BGRA at the input's size. If the name is taken, the sender becomes `name_1`, `name_2`, ...; the published name shows in the parameter panel. A Share In in the same project can receive it (a loopback): give the Share Out a name of its own and type it into the Share In. Spout needs the DX12 graphics backend (elsewhere the node reports an error); Syphon on macOS is not wired yet.
- [Slope](img/slope.md) — The luminance gradient: red = horizontal slope, green = vertical slope, around mid-grey.
- [Switch](img/switch.md) — Outputs one of up to four inputs, optionally blending between neighbours.
- [Text](img/text.md) — Draws text (several lines allowed) over the input or a background colour, in a built-in or TrueType/OpenType font.
- [Threshold](img/threshold.md) — White where a channel is above the threshold, black elsewhere.
- [Tile](img/tile.md) — Repeats the input in a grid.
- [Time Machine](img/time_machine.md) — Shows each pixel of `in` from a different moment: the luminance of `time` (black = now, white = oldest) picks how many frames back, blending between frames. Without `time`, the whole image is delayed by Offset.
- [Transform](img/transform.md) — Translates, rotates and scales the input around a pivot.
- [WGSL](img/wgsl.md) — A full-screen shader you write in WGSL: the body of `fn shade(uv: vec2f) -> vec4f` (uv from the top-left). Inputs are `in0` and `in1` with sampler `samp`; `p1()`…`p4()` return the four parameters, and `time()`, `resolution()` and `frame()` are available. A file reloads whenever it is saved; the last working shader keeps running while you edit.

## SIG (29)

- [Audio File In](sig/audio_file_in.md) — Plays a WAV file as audio-rate channels (`left`/`right`, or `mono`). Decoding runs off the render thread.
- [Audio In](sig/audio_in.md) — Live audio from an input device as audio-rate channels (`left`/`right`, `mono`, or `chan1`…). The device opens and runs on its own thread.
- [Audio Out](sig/audio_out.md) — Plays audio-rate channels (from Audio In, Audio File In or audio processing) on an output device; mono feeds every speaker, otherwise channels map in order. Converts to the device rate; passes the input through.
- [Beat](sig/beat.md) — A tempo clock: `beat` count, `beat_phase` 0–1 within the beat, `bar` count and `bar_phase`.
- [Constant](sig/constant.md) — Up to four named channels with constant values.
- [Count](sig/count.md) — Counts how many times the input rises above a threshold. Outputs `count`.
- [DMX Out](sig/dmx_out.md) — Sends input channels (0–1) as DMX512 slots from Start, over Art-Net or sACN (E1.31). The universe is sent when it changes (at most 44 times a second) and repeated every second.
- [Envelope](sig/envelope.md) — Follows the loudness of an audio input per channel (RMS or peak) with attack and release.
- [Filter](sig/filter.md) — One-pole low-pass (smooth) or high-pass (changes only) filter per channel.
- [Input Devices](sig/input.md) — Mouse, keyboard or gamepad as channels. Mouse: position over the viewed image (0–1, y up), buttons and wheel. Keyboard: 1 while each listed key is held. Gamepad: sticks, triggers and buttons.
- [Lag](sig/lag.md) — Smooths changes with separate rise and fall times.
- [LFO](sig/lfo.md) — A low-frequency oscillator: sine, triangle, square or ramp over time. Outputs channel `lfo`.
- [Limit](sig/limit.md) — Clamps channels to a range, optionally quantising to a step.
- [Math](sig/math.md) — Combines A with B channel by channel, then maps the range from→to.
- [Merge](sig/merge.md) — Combines the channels of up to four inputs, in input order.
- [MIDI In](sig/midi_in.md) — Channels from a MIDI input: controllers (`ch1_cc7`), notes (velocity while held, 0 after release), pitch bend, pressure and program, added as they arrive. The port runs on its own thread.
- [MIDI Out](sig/midi_out.md) — Sends each input channel (0–1) as a MIDI control change, numbered from First CC, whenever its value changes; passes the input through.
- [Noise](sig/noise.md) — Smooth random channels that wander over time.
- [Onset](sig/onset.md) — Detects onsets (beats, hits) in an audio input by spectral flux. Outputs `onset` (1 on detection frames) and `flux`.
- [OSC In](sig/osc_in.md) — Receives OSC on a UDP port; each numeric address becomes a channel (`/a/b` → `a_b`) holding its latest value.
- [OSC Out](sig/osc_out.md) — Sends each input channel as an OSC float message `<prefix>/<channel>` whenever the input changes, and passes it through.
- [Pattern](sig/pattern.md) — One channel of Length samples shaped by a waveform over Cycles; Speed scrolls it over time. Feed Channel Image to turn it into a texture, or index it from other operators.
- [Select](sig/select.md) — Keeps only the named channels.
- [Spectrum](sig/spectrum.md) — Band energies of an audio input (log-spaced `band1`…`bandN`, a full-scale sine reads about 1) plus its RMS `level`.
- [Speed](sig/speed.md) — Integrates each channel over time: the input is a rate per second.
- [Timecode In](sig/timecode_in.md) — SMPTE timecode from MIDI Time Code or from LTC audio. `time` is the position in seconds and keeps moving between updates; `locked` is 1 while code is arriving. The input runs on its own thread.
- [Timer](sig/timer.md) — Counts seconds while playing: `seconds`, `fraction` of the length and completed `cycles`.
- [Trail](sig/trail.md) — Keeps the recent history of each channel: each output channel holds the last N frames, oldest first.
- [Trigger](sig/trigger.md) — An attack/decay envelope fired when the input rises above a threshold or Fire is switched on. Outputs `trigger`.

## PNT (34)

- [Age](pnt/age.md) — Ages points and removes them by rules: `age += dt` each frame (created at zero when missing), then removes points that reached their `life` (or the Life parameter when they have none), points inside or outside a region, and points slower than a speed. For simulation loops (Solver, or a feedback chain); Particles ages and removes its own particles.
- [Attract](pnt/attract.md) — A force toward a point (or away from it with a negative strength) on the velocity `v` (created at zero when missing), reading `P`: a spring, a constant pull or an inverse-square pull, optionally fading out with distance.
- [Attribute](pnt/attribute.md) — Creates or sets a point attribute of any type: a constant, a ramp between two values along an axis, a ramp over the point numbers, or gradient noise at `P`. An attribute of another type with the same name is replaced.
- [Attribute Math](pnt/attribute_math.md) — Computes a point attribute from another, component by component: Result = A (op) B, where B is a second attribute or a constant. Arithmetic, min/max, power, mix, fit (remap a range), clamp, and one-input functions (length, normalize, abs, negate, floor, fract, sin, cos, set).
- [Bend](pnt/bend.md) — Bends `P` around an arc: the stretch of Length along Axis from Origin curves toward Direction through Angle degrees; beyond it points continue straight along the bent end.
- [Collide](pnt/collide.md) — Keeps points out of a plane or a sphere (or inside a sphere): a point that has passed the surface is moved back onto it and its velocity `v` is reflected, scaled by Bounce, with Friction taking off sliding speed. Writes `P` and `v` (created at zero when missing).
- [Colour by Attribute](pnt/colour.md) — Colours points from an attribute: a component (or the length) of it, mapped from [Low, High] to 0-1, picks a colour from a ramp (two or three colours of your choice, or the heat, ice and rainbow presets) written to `Cd`.
- [Connect](pnt/connect.md) — Links each point to its nearest neighbours within Radius with lines (polyline primitives of two points; Render Points draws them with Draw set to lines), and writes `near` (the nearest neighbour's number, or the point's own) and `neardist` (its distance, or Radius when there is none). Neighbours come from a GPU grid hash.
- [Copy to Points](pnt/copy.md) — Copies the `source` geometry onto every point of `points`: copy t of the source is scaled by Scale and the point's `pscale`, turned by its `orient` (a quaternion) or so the source's +Z follows its `N`, and moved to its `P`. Every source attribute is copied; `copynum` gives the copy's number, and `Cd` can take the point's colour.
- [Delete](pnt/delete.md) — Removes the points a rule selects: members of a group, points inside a box or sphere, or points whose attribute passes a comparison. Invert keeps them instead, so two Deletes split a geometry. Every attribute follows; the count stays on the GPU.
- [Drag](pnt/drag.md) — Slows the velocity `v` down: linear drag (a fraction of the speed lost per second, frame-rate independent) or quadratic drag (stronger at high speed).
- [Emitter](pnt/emitter.md) — Emits particles from the points of `source` into the particle state on `in` (for example a Particles system with rate 0, in its feedback loop): Rate per second from random source points, with a velocity, a speed along the source's normal `N`, a share of its velocity `v` and a random spread. Writes `P`, `v`, `age`, `life`, `id` (and `Cd` when the state has it) of the new particles; the state's capacity limits the count.
- [Gravity](pnt/gravity.md) — A constant acceleration on the velocity `v` (created at zero when missing).
- [Group](pnt/group.md) — Puts points into a named group by a rule (inside a box or sphere, an attribute comparison, a random fraction, every Nth, a range, or another group), as a bit of the `group` attribute. Delete, and other operators' group options, read it.
- [Integrate](pnt/integrate.md) — Moves points by their velocity for one frame: `P += v × dt × scale`. The step of a home-made simulation (Solver, or a feedback loop without Particles); Particles integrates by itself.
- [Merge](pnt/merge.md) — Combines up to four geometries into one: their points one after the other (a, then b, c, d). Attributes are the union of the inputs'; points from an input without one get zero (an attribute whose type differs from the first input's is treated as missing). Groups are not carried; primitives are dropped.
- [Noise](pnt/noise.md) — Curl noise, a swirling divergence-free field: displaces `P`, or adds a force to the velocity `v` (created at zero when missing) so particles flow. Animate it through `evolution` (for example the expression `time * 0.3`).
- [Normals](pnt/normals.md) — Estimates surface normals `N` for a point cloud: the direction in which each point's neighbours (within Radius) spread least, from their covariance, turned outward from a centre or toward a direction. Neighbours come from a GPU grid hash.
- [Particles](pnt/particles.md) — A GPU particle system: emits particles at a rate, ages them, removes them at the end of their life and moves them with curl noise, attraction to a point, gravity and drag. Writes `P`, `v`, `age`, `life`, `id` and `Cd`; the live count stays on the GPU. Wire the end of a chain of force operators into `prev` to carry their effect to the next frame.
- [Piece](pnt/piece.md) — For-Each Begin: numbers points by piece into a `piece` attribute, so the operators after it act per piece (Randomize keyed by `piece` gives every piece its own value; Group, Delete, Sort and Colour by Attribute read it too): chunks of Size consecutive points, buckets of an attribute's value (for example `copynum` from Copy to Points), or cells of a spatial grid.
- [Points](pnt/points.md) — A point source: a grid, random points in a box, a line or a circle, made on the GPU. Writes `P`, `Cd` (colour) and `id` (0, 1, 2, …).
- [Randomize](pnt/randomize.md) — Sets a point attribute to seeded random values: uniform between Min and Max per component, normal (Gaussian, mean Min, deviation Max), or random unit directions scaled by Max. A function of the seed and each point's `id` (or number), so the same points get the same values on every run and frame.
- [Scatter](pnt/scatter.md) — Random points, uniformly distributed on a shape: a line segment, a circle or a disk, a rectangle, a sphere's surface, a ball or a box. Writes `P`, `id` and, on the flat and spherical shapes, the surface normal `N`. Seeded: the same seed gives the same points. (Scattering on a mesh surface comes with the MSH operators.)
- [Smooth](pnt/smooth.md) — Averages a point attribute with its neighbours within Radius, weighted by distance (closer counts more): relaxes positions, blurs colours or any other attribute over a point cloud. Neighbours come from a GPU grid hash of the input positions.
- [Solver](pnt/solver.md) — The entry of a simulation loop: outputs `init` at the start (first frame, Reset, until Start Frame, or while `prev` has no geometry), then the previous frame's state from `prev`. Wire the end of the body (for example Gravity → Collide → Integrate → Age) into `prev`; the body may sit in a Pack (a Solver Block). The state is updated in place when nothing else reads it.
- [Sort](pnt/sort.md) — Reorders points: along an axis, by distance from a point (far first suits Render Points' `over` blending), by an attribute, randomly (seeded) or reversed. Equal keys keep their order, so the result is the same on every run. Every attribute follows; a few milliseconds for millions of points (not a per-frame step at that size).
- [Subset](pnt/subset.md) — Keeps a subset of the points: one in every Nth, a seeded random fraction, or a range of point numbers (by `id` when the points have one, so particles keep their fate). Every attribute follows; the count stays on the GPU.
- [Taper](pnt/taper.md) — Scales `P` across an axis by an amount that changes along it: Start at the origin to End at one Length along the axis (held beyond).
- [Transform](pnt/transform.md) — Translates, rotates and scales points about a pivot: moves `P`, and turns `v` (rotation and scale) and `N` (kept perpendicular to surfaces, unit length) when they exist.
- [Turbulence](pnt/turbulence.md) — Fractal curl noise (several octaves of the curl noise `pnt.noise` uses) as a force on the velocity `v`: swirls at several scales, divergence-free, so points mix without bunching up.
- [Twist](pnt/twist.md) — Twists `P` about an axis: points turn by an angle that grows along the axis, Angle degrees per Length.
- [Vortex](pnt/vortex.md) — A swirl about an axis on the velocity `v` (reading `P`): spins points around the axis, optionally pulls them in toward it and lifts them along it, fading out with distance from the axis.
- [Wave](pnt/wave.md) — Displaces `P` by a sine wave: along Direction, with the wave travelling along Travel (linear) or outward from Origin in the plane across Travel (radial, ripples). Animate Phase (degrees) to make it move.
- [Wind](pnt/wind.md) — Air moving at a velocity, with gusts from curl noise: the velocity `v` relaxes toward the wind's at the response rate (reading `P` for the gusts).

## MSH (27)

- [Boolean](msh/boolean.md) — Union, intersection or difference of two closed meshes (BSP trees). The result is welded; pieces from A and B are the groups `from_a` and `from_b`. Up to 200,000 triangles per input; seams may keep T-junctions; normals are not written (add Normals).
- [Box](msh/box.md) — A closed box of quads, welded at its edges, with smooth point normals `N` (add Normals in flat mode for hard edges).
- [Circle](msh/circle.md) — A circle or arc: one polygon, an outline (polyline) or points, in a chosen plane.
- [Colour](msh/colour.md) — Writes the point colour `Cd`: a constant, or from the normals, texture coordinates, position in the bounds, or a seeded random colour per point or per primitive, times a tint.
- [Delete](msh/delete.md) — Deletes the primitives or points of a group (or everything outside it). Deleting points deletes the primitives that use them.
- [Extrude](msh/extrude.md) — Pushes polygons out along their normals and adds side walls, each polygon on its own (with an inset) or as connected regions. The moved polygons are the group `extrude_front`, the walls `extrude_side`.
- [Mesh File In](msh/file_in.md) — Reads a static mesh from an OBJ, PLY or glTF 2.0 (.gltf, .glb) file: positions, polygons and lines, normals, texture coordinates and colours (glTF: the default scene with its node transforms).
- [Mesh File Out](msh/file_out.md) — Writes its input to an OBJ, PLY or glTF 2.0 (.gltf, .glb) file whenever it changes, and passes it through. Files are byte-identical for the same geometry.
- [Fuse](msh/fuse.md) — Welds points closer than a distance into one and removes primitives that collapse.
- [Grid](msh/grid.md) — A flat rectangle of quads (a plane), with normals `N` and texture coordinates `uv`.
- [Group](msh/group.md) — Makes a point or primitive group: elements inside a box, facing a direction, every n-th in an index range, or all; combined with an existing group by union, intersection or subtraction.
- [Iso Surface](msh/iso_surface.md) — Builds a closed mesh where a field crosses zero (marching tetrahedra): blended spheres around the input points (radius × `pscale`), or an analytic sphere, box, torus or gyroid. Writes `N` from the field.
- [Line](msh/line.md) — A straight polyline between two points, with evenly spaced points (a curve for Sweep).
- [Merge](msh/merge.md) — Combines up to four geometries into one. Attributes and groups are joined by name; missing values are zero (one for `Cd` and `pscale`).
- [Normals](msh/normals.md) — Computes normals `N`: smooth point normals with a cusp angle that keeps sharp edges, flat (faceted) normals, or one normal per primitive.
- [Platonic Solid](msh/platonic.md) — A tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, dodecahedron or icosahedron centred on Centre, or a flat regular polygon facing +Z, as polygons wound counter-clockwise seen from outside. Each face has its own points, so it writes flat normals `N` and per-face texture coordinates `uv` (the polygon's span the unit square); optionally a colour per face (prim `Cd`).
- [PolyReduce](msh/polyreduce.md) — Reduces the polygon count while keeping the shape: quadric error edge collapses on triangles, down to a share of the triangles or until the error passes a tolerance. Borders can be kept.
- [Scatter](msh/scatter.md) — Seeded random points on the surface, evenly by area, with the surface normal `N`, an `id` and the surface's other attributes interpolated: points for PNT operators or Iso Surface.
- [Skin](msh/skin.md) — Lofts a surface through the input's primitives in order (profiles with equal point counts), optionally as a closed ring and with end caps.
- [Sphere](msh/sphere.md) — A closed sphere: polar rings of quads, or an evenly subdivided icosahedron. Writes `N`.
- [Subdivide](msh/subdivide.md) — Smooths polygons by Catmull-Clark subdivision: each level splits every polygon into quads and rounds the shape (open borders stay as curves). Attributes are interpolated.
- [Sweep](msh/sweep.md) — Sweeps a profile along a curve: the first primitive of `profile` (drawn in the XY plane) or a circle, along the first primitive of `curve`, with rotation-minimising frames, scale and twist. Writes `N` and `uv`.
- [Torus](msh/torus.md) — A closed torus (ring) around Y, made of quads, with normals `N`.
- [Transform](msh/transform.md) — Scales, rotates and moves the geometry about a pivot. Normals follow; a mirror flips polygons so they still face outward.
- [Triangulate](msh/triangulate.md) — Splits every polygon into triangles (ear clipping, so concave polygons work). Polylines are kept.
- [Tube](msh/tube.md) — A cylinder or cone along Y, optionally capped (closed), with smooth normals `N`. A radius of 0 makes a cone point.
- [UV Project](msh/uv_project.md) — Writes texture coordinates `uv` per point by a planar, cylindrical, spherical or box projection, fitted to the bounds.

## MAT (6)

- [Line](mat/line.md) — Wireframe: the edges of every polygon (not the diagonals the renderer adds to draw it) and every polyline, antialiased, a set number of pixels wide; inside the polygons a fill colour, or nothing so the back edges show.
- [Matcap](mat/matcap.md) — A lit-sphere image (`matcap` input) looked up by the surface normal as the camera sees it, so a painted or photographed sphere gives the whole shape its look with no lights. Without an image it shows a soft top light.
- [PBR](mat/pbr.md) — A lit surface: Lambert diffuse plus GGX specular from the renderer's light, ambient light and emission. Albedo is multiplied by `Cd` and by the `map` image (at `uv`) when they are there; emission adds a glow that lighting does not darken.
- [Point Sprite](mat/point_sprite.md) — Draws the geometry's points (not its polygons) as discs or squares facing the camera, sized in world units (scaled by `pscale` when present, and by perspective) or in pixels; optionally shaded like small spheres by the renderer's light.
- [Toon](mat/toon.md) — Cel shading: the light's diffuse term in a few hard bands between Shadow and Colour, a hard highlight and a rim light along silhouettes.
- [Unlit](mat/unlit.md) — A flat colour with no lighting: Colour, times `Cd` and times the `map` image (sampled at the `uv` attribute) when they are there. Use it for constant colours, vertex colours and textures shown as they are.
